# Mutations: design reference

Generated from the code: every base mutation, its abilities, and its secondary mutation list.

## How it works

- **Omega Awakening:** at mastery level 75 each base mutation unlocks an ultimate on its own key (Z) with a 10-minute cooldown. See the table in README.md.
- On first join you roll one random **base mutation** (permanent).
- Mastery XP comes from kills and from using **active** abilities (passives give none). No level cap; +3 stat points per level.
- At mastery **10, 25 and 50** you're offered **3 random secondaries** from your base's list and pick one (max 3).
- Stats: Strength, Vitality, Agility, Endurance, Luck, Mutation. No caps. Respec costs 30 XP levels.

- **Downside:** Hunger drains much faster.
- **[A] Surge** (30s): Burst-heal 4 hearts. Costs 6 hunger.
- **[A] Immunity** (180s): Completely immune to damage for 8 seconds.

| Thick Hide | +4 armor. |

- **[A] Force Push** (8s): Blast mobs in front of you back. Throws held blocks.

- **Upside:** Immune to lightning. Faster in the rain.
- **Downside:** All of your abilities are weaker in clear weather.
- **[A] Lightning Strike** (6s): Call lightning down where you're looking.
- **[A] Summon Storm** (1200s): Turn the weather into a thunderstorm.

| Secondary | Effect |

| Rainwalker | Fly in the rain (double-tap jump, hold forward to soar where you look). |
| Charged Up | Being struck by lightning heals you and speeds you up. |
| Gale Step | Jump much higher in rain and storms. |
| [A] Thunderclap | Knock back everything around you. |
| Eye of the Storm | Night vision while it's raining. |

- **Downside:** You can't wear anything in your chest slot. Top speeds burn hunger.

- **[A] Speed Punch** (8s): A blur of punches on the mob in front of you. More hits at higher speed tiers.
- **[A] Vibrate** (20s): Vibrate so fast nothing can touch you for 2 seconds, knocking nearby mobs away.
- **[A] Phase Through** (25s): For 6 seconds, walk through walls (never through the floor).

- **Downside:** Weakened in hot biomes and the Nether (overworld heat doesn't bother you at night).

- **[A] Charged Leap** (1s): Spend 5 charge to leap 10 blocks up.

- **[A] Copycat** (90s): Borrow a trait from the last mob type you killed.

## Pyro
- **Upside:** In Flame On: immune to fire and lava. Sneak + right-click a lava bucket to drink it (heals like a golden apple).
- **Downside:** In Flame On: water and rain hurt you.
- **[A] Flamethrower** (10s): Spray fire in front of you for 4 seconds. Lights and fuels furnaces.
- **[A] Flame Flight** (45s): Fly on jets of fire for 10 seconds.
- **[A] Flame On** (0.5s): Toggle: become living flame. All your Pyro powers and drawbacks (except Phoenix) only work while it's on.

| Secondary | Effect |

## Host
- **Upside:** A living organism with tendrils: extra reach, wall climbing, stronger after a kill. Bond with it for full power.
- **Downside:** It's always hungry: go 10 minutes without a kill and it feeds on you. Takes 75% less fall damage.
- **[A] Tendril** (4s): Yank a mob to you, or pull yourself to a block.
- **[A] Bond** (0.5s): Toggle: let the organism fully bond with you. Full power, but no armor and it hungers twice as fast.
- **Dormant vs bonded:** dormant = faint look, small tendrils, weaker Tendril (8 blocks) and Lash (3 blocks, half damage), +0.75 reach, slow climbing. Bonded = full Warden-like look, big tendrils that grip walls, full-power abilities, +1.5 reach, Strength I + Resistance I, Jump Boost V, Speed II; no armor and the hunger clock runs 2x.

| Secondary | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hungry Tendrils | Tendril cooldown halved. |
| Symbiote Armor | While bonded, Resistance II instead of Resistance I. |
| Devour | Kills restore hunger. |
| Wall Crawler | Climb faster, and hold jump to cling to ceilings. |
| Shapeless | Sneak into a 1-block gap to squeeze through it. |
| [A] Lash | Whip every mob around you. |
| Well Fed | The organism can go 15 minutes between kills instead of 10. |
| Parasite | Your hits heal you for 20% of the damage. |
